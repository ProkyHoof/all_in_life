# 写作题型概述
***
## 写作原则
1. 保底原则
	1. 单词正确
	2. 语法正确
		1. 我爱你是显而易见的。*It is obvious that I love you. 或者 I love you. It is obvious.*
		2. 站在我前面的那个男生看起来很帅。*The boy who is standing in front of me is handsome. 或者 A boy is standing in front of me. He looks handsome.*
2. 高分原则
	1. 语言准确多变
		“认为”：*think, believe, claim, assert, contend, argue, assume, presure*
		“人们”：*people, individuals, students, young men, adults*
		"喜欢"：*like, be fond of, be keen on, have a passion for*
	2. 结构体现逻辑
		1. 表示因果
			1. 表示原因
				*because, in that, as, since, because of, dut to, owing to, thanks to*
			2. 表示结果
				*so, hence, therefore, thereby, thus, consequently, in consequence, as a result/consequence*
		2. 并列关系
			*and, likewise, similarly, equally, simultaneously, at the same time, in the meanwhile, in the same way*
		3. 表示对比
			*but, by contrast, conversely, alternatively, on the contrary, on the other hand, otherwise, while, whereas*
		4. 表示递进
			*besides, also, in addition, moreover, furthermore, apart/aside from this*
		5. 表示让步
			*despite, in spite of, although, though, as, while, even if, much as（用于句首）, granted that undoubtedly, there is no doubt that... （毫无疑问的是）, it may be true that... （或许真实的是）, there is no denying that... （不可否认的是）, in effect/fact/reality, virtually, indeed（实际上，事实上，的确）*
		6. 表示引出新对象
			*about, concerning, regarding, with regard to, as for/to, in respect of（关于）,in terms of（就而论，在...方面）, where it comes to... （当提到...时）*
		7. 表示总结
			*on the whole, all in all, overall, in conclusion/summary/general, generally(speaking), as a whole, mainly, substantially, to sum up, come to the conclusion, in short/brief, in a word*
		`e.g.`我爱你，我决定嫁给你。
		*I love you, so I will marry you.*
		`e.g.`我不想背单词，我又想通过四级考试。
		*I don't want to memorize words, but I want to pass CET-4.*
	3. 内容完整扣题
		1. 简单句
		`e.g.`重庆江北机场就要到了。
		*We are arriving at Chongqing Jianbei Airport.*
		`e.g.`全球在变暖。
		*It is becoming warm throughout the world.*
		`e.g.`前面在修路。
		*A road is being built in front.*
		`e.g.`狗没有找到。
		*The dog was not found./I didn't find your dog.*
		`e.g.`妒忌本身就是一种仰望。
		*Jealousy is a kind of worship./Being jealous is a kind of worship.*
		`e.g.`我老公每天上网，花大量时间打游戏、看小视频。
		*My husband surfs the Internet every day, spending a lot of time playing games and watching short videos.*
		`e.g.`舞会结束了，大雁失望地回家了。
		*The party was over and Dayan went home disappointed.*

		*Wuzhen is a water town in Zhejiang Province, lying near a river from Beijing to Hangzhou.*
		`e.g.`唐代，灯笼在很多地方流行起来，它象征着生活美满，生意兴隆。
		*Lanterns became popular in many places in Tang Dynasty, symbolizing a happy life and prosperous business.*

## 议论文模板
> 注意：只有第一段不一样，第二三段是一样的。议论文，最主要的三种模板，都是三个段落。

1. **现象解释类**
	第一段：rapid development
	*With the continuous/rapid advancement/development of _(填：society, culture, medical, service, education, economy), it is of great necessity for _(填：students, residents, citizens, youngsters) to _. It is quite natural for us to ponder over the question for _(填：主题词).*
	翻译：随着__（社会、文化、医疗服务、教育、经济）的发展，对于__（学生、居民、公民、年轻人）来说我们应该__。很自然的我们应该思考__问题。
2. **观点选择类**
	第一段：
	*With the continuous advancement of _(填：society, culture, medical, service, education, economy), contemporary _（一般填：student, citizens, indiciduals） face a diverse array of pathways upon _（主题词）. Some advocate that pursuing _（选择A） yields significant advantages, whereas others contend that opting for _（选择B） represents a superior choice. From my perspective, I endorse the former. The following reasons can account for my preference.*
	翻译：在__的加速进程中，当代__在__后面临多种选择，一些人主张追求__具有显示优势，而另一些人则认为选择__是更优的选项。我个人倾向于前者。
