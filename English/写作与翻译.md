# 写作题型概述
***
## 写作类型
1. 保底原则
	1. 单词正确
	2. 语法正确
		1. 我爱你是显而易见的。*It is obvious that I love you. 或者 I love you. It is obvious.*
		2. 站在我前面的那个男生看起来很帅。*The boy who is standing in front of me is handsome. 或者 A boy is standing in front of me. He looks handsome.*
2. 高分原则
	1. 语言准确多变
		“认为”：*think, believe, claim, assert, contend, argue, assume, presure*
		“人们”：*people, individuals, students, young men, adults*
		"喜欢"：*like, be fond of, be keen on, have a passion for*
	2. 结构体现逻辑
		1. 表示因果
			1. 表示原因
				*because, in that, as, since, because of, dut to, owing to, thanks to*
			2. 表示结果
				*so, hence, therefore, thereby, thus, consequently, in consequence, as a result/consequence*
		2. 并列关系
			*and, likewise, similarly, e*
